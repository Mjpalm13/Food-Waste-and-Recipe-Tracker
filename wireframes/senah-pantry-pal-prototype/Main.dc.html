About this prototype

This is an early, low-fidelity version of Pantry Pal. Boxes with an X are where photos will go. Colors, words and layouts will change.

What worksAdding and removing pantry items, finding and filtering recipes, saving, writing your own recipe, cooking step by step, and sharing.
What is pretendThe camera scanner, photos, real friends and sending. Nothing you do is saved after you close the page.
