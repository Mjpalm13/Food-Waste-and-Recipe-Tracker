# Food-Waste-and-Recipe-Tracker
Design System Inspiration 
-------------------------
Our vision is to have a warm, inviting, engaging app that is clean cut but still cutesy. We like the green color palette with warm, neutral colors
- we want to include actual icons or animations, not emojis (think Dudel drawings or Yuka carrot)
** YUKA
- we like the carrot design
- like the color palette
- do not like how iOS everything looks
- first few pages are ok, but not great
- do not like the fonts, feel childish

** FIG
- we like how clean cut Fig is while not being too cold
- we don't love the color palette but the yellow isn't too bad (?) it's kind of garish though
- fonts are great and we love them
- feels a little too basic

** HELLO FRESH
- we like the green and neutral color palette
- we like the icons that are attached and not emojis
- the font is cleancut
- the green color on top of nav bar is kind of intense and not as warm

** BLUE APRON
- has the most screens that we felt like were cohesive
- off white and tan color feels warmer and not bland / stark
- we like the suggested buttons and tiles
- food pictures on second page look clean
- we do not like how professional it feels / the professional font
- we like how the name is Blue Apron (with cooking) and has a blue/purple color scheme
